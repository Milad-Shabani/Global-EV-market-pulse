Trade facts, 2025

    Battery supply chain

    The battery race is even more concentrated than the car race

    Over 80% of the world's battery cells are produced in China — and nearly all the rest come from Korea or Japan.

    Share of global battery-cell production, 2025

    Why it matters

    China accounted for over 80% of battery-cell production in 2025, and an even higher share of the active materials that go into EV batteries. Nearly all battery cells worldwide are supplied by companies headquartered in China, Korea or Japan — a concentration that shapes pricing, trade policy and industrial strategy across every other market in this dashboard.

    Charging infrastructure

    7 million+ public chargers — and China built most of them

    Most EV owners charge privately, but public charging determines whether an EV works for everyone else. China alone accounts for more than 65% of the world's public charging points.

              Methodology & sources

              Where every number comes from

              This dashboard is generated by a Python script (data/generate_workbook.py) straight from figures published in the report below, and ships both as this dashboard's data file and as the downloadable Excel workbook.

              Primary source

                Notes on estimates

                Figures marked estimate in the workbook are back-calculated from a share the report states applied to a reported regional total (for example, "UAE ≈ 50% of the Middle East's ~75,000 2025 sales"). 2020–2024 in the global trend chart are reconstructed from the report's stated pattern of roughly 3.5 million additional sales every year since 2021, anchored to the confirmed 2025 and 2026 figures. Every other number is stated directly in the report.
